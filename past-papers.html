Egypt, paper versions & missing sessions
  • The right course: British Council Egypt lists P1 as WMA11 and P2 as WMA12. This archive defaults to standard /01 papers. Regional /01R and /01A papers are kept separate for extra practice; the public Egypt list does not identify regional suffix allocations. Your centre’s entry information is the final check.   • 2018 specimen: Pearson supplies the question papers and mark schemes together in one sample-assessment booklet. The buttons jump to the relevant PDF pages; some PDF viewers may open at the beginning instead.
  • January 2019: P1 had started, but P2’s first assessment was June 2019. There is no January 2019 P2 paper. Pearson specification ↗
  • 2020: The summer exams were cancelled. Pearson reused summer material in October, so a summer date on an October 2020 paper is expected. Pearson’s explanation ↗
  • Summer 2021: These are the assessment materials Pearson lists under June 2021, from the alternative-assessment period, not a normal summer exam sitting. The archive uses the final mark schemes, not the provisional versions. Pearson’s 2021 guidance ↗
  • Recent papers: Pearson currently restricts October 2025 and January/June 2026 materials to registered centres. Their entries link to the official catalogue. October 2026 has not yet taken place as of this archive’s review date.
